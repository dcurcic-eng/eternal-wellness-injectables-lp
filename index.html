SAFETY, HONESTLY

What we tell every patient.

Board-certified medical oversight on every treatment. Here is the honest side effect profile, before you book rather than after.

COMMON AND TEMPORARY

Mild redness at the injection sites, settling within minutes to a few hours.

Slight swelling or a small bruise where the needle entered, usually gone in one to two days.

A mild headache on the day of treatment, which over-the-counter relief handles.

LESS COMMON

Temporary brow or eyelid heaviness, in roughly 1–5% of patients.

Slight asymmetry as the product distributes through the muscle.

Both resolve on their own. We review you at two weeks either way and adjust if needed.

Not suitable if

We screen for all of these at consultation. If any apply, we will tell you and we will not treat.

You are pregnant or breastfeeding.

You have a known allergy to botulinum toxin type A.

There is an active skin infection at the injection site.

You live with a neuromuscular condition - myasthenia gravis, Lambert-Eaton syndrome or ALS.

AFTERCARE

No strenuous exercise for 4–6 hours, don't rub or massage the treated area, and stay upright for the first 4–6 hours. Everything else resumes the same day - there is no downtime.
